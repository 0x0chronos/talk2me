Spanish speech recognition uses the Vosk small Spanish model 0.42 (Alpha Cephei)
and the WebAssembly worker from vosk-browser 0.0.8 (Ciaran O'Reilly).
Both are distributed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (LICENSE.txt).
The model is repackaged as a tar archive. The worker is served as a separate
file and marks its final response when a recognizer is removed.
https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models
https://www.npmjs.com/package/vosk-browser/v/0.0.8
